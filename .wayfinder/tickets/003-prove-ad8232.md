# 003 — Prove the AD8232 makes a usable trace

`wayfinder:task` · HITL · Status: **needs a human with the hardware in hand**

## Question

No BLE, no app. Stream raw ADC over USB serial, dump 30 s to CSV, run it through
`ml/reference/dsp_reference.analyse_sqi`. The only hardware question that matters: does
the rig clear our own quality gate?

Watch for the two classic first-build failures, both of which present as "the app rejects
every capture": the RL reference electrode omitted (floating input), and mains hum from a
plugged-in laptop. Simpler now than originally scoped — one I²C device on the bus, not
two, since the MPU-6050 is dropped.

**Also capture a deliberately disturbed trace** — patient shifting, arm moving. With the
IMU gone, this recording is the ground truth ticket 006 (inferred motion gate) needs to
choose a threshold honestly, rather than guessing.

Blocked by: none — frontier, but requires the human operator, not just the agent.
Blocks: 006's threshold choice (fog item), 013 (ESP32 firmware).
